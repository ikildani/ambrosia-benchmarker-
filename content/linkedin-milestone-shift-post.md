# LinkedIn Post: Where the Money Actually Goes

## POST TEXT (from Issa's personal LinkedIn profile)

Every BD team I talk to asks the same question:

"What's the total deal value?"

Wrong question.

A $2B Phase 2 deal puts $300M upfront. Milestones tied to development.

A $2B approved deal puts $600M upfront. Milestones tied to revenue.

Same headline. Completely different bet.

We see this across 3,500+ biopharma transactions:

At discovery, 55% of milestones are development.
At approval, 5%.

11x shift. Same line item on the term sheet.

Early-stage deals price the science.
Late-stage deals price the sales.

And the sharpest inflection? Phase 2. Upfronts nearly double after proof-of-concept. That's the single most undervalued moment in deal timing.

If you're only benchmarking on total deal value, you're negotiating blind.

What's the first thing you look at on a term sheet?
