# LinkedIn Post: TA Upfront Ratios — Thursday

## POST TEXT (from Issa's personal LinkedIn profile)

Oncology is the biggest market in biopharma.

It also has the worst deal economics for licensors.

92% of hematology deal value is paid upfront. Cash at close.

Oncology? 25%. The rest is milestones — regulatory, commercial, sales targets. Events that may never happen.

I looked at the upfront ratio across every major therapeutic area in 3,500+ deals. Oncology is dead last.

Not by a little. By 3.6x.

Here's what that means in practice:

You're a biotech founder with a Phase 2 oncology asset. A pharma company offers you a "$2B deal." You celebrate. But $1.5B of that is milestones you'll never control.

Your friend in hematology gets a "$2B deal." She walks away with $1.8B in the bank.

Same press release. Completely different outcome.

The therapeutic area you're in doesn't just determine your market — it determines how much of the deal you actually get paid.

If you could choose your TA purely on deal economics, would you still pick oncology?
