# LinkedIn Post: What 353 Disclosed Royalty Rates Tell You Before You Negotiate

Data: `deals_verified`, licensing deals announced 2019-01-01 to 2026-09-28 (n = 1,083). Pulled 2026-10-14.

- Royalty rate disclosed: 353 (33%); upfront disclosed: 793 (73%)
- Disclosure check: of 448 no-rate deals with stored source text, 220 mention royalties and only 12
  contain a rate we missed, so the gap is real non-disclosure, not extraction loss
- Tier spread (both ends disclosed, n = 277): median top minus bottom = 5 points; top >= 2x bottom in 45%
- Upfront vs royalty, deals split at each phase by upfront share of total (median top tier,
  low-upfront half vs high-upfront half): preclinical 9 vs 9 (20/20), Phase 1 12 vs 12 (11/11),
  Phase 2 15 vs 19 (11/11), Phase 3 19 vs 22 (17/17), approved 20 vs 19 (21/20)
- Late-stage (Phase 3 + approved) median top tier by territory: worldwide 11.75% (n = 16),
  China 16% (17), US 16% (10), Japan 23.5% (8); "regional" bucket 19.5% (34)

Samples for points 2 and 3 are small; the first comment says so.

## POST TEXT (from Issa's personal LinkedIn profile)

Only a third of biopharma licensing deals disclose their royalty rate.

We went through the 353 that did since 2019. Three things I'd want to know before negotiating one:

1. "Up to 19%" is the top tier, and many products never reach it. The bottom tier was a median of 5 points lower, and in almost half the deals the top rate was at least double the bottom. Negotiate where the sales thresholds sit, not just the rates.

2. Taking less upfront doesn't seem to buy a better royalty. Deals that paid more upfront had the same or higher royalties at almost every stage. At Phase 2, the median top tier was 19% for upfront-heavy deals and 15% for the rest.

3. Use comps for the same rights. Late-stage deals for a single market like Japan, China or the US mostly topped out between 16% and 24%. Worldwide deals came in around 12%. Global comps undersell a regional deal.

For those who've negotiated these: which royalty term do you wish you'd pushed harder on?
