2 Executive Summary

Market Overview — PCC-to-IND Licensing, 2020–2026

The preclinical out-licensing market has seen a structural shift: upfronts are growing as Big Pharma competes earlier for differentiated assets, particularly in obesity/metabolic, autoimmune, and oncology. This report benchmarks the current state of pricing across the modalities and therapeutic areas most relevant to Daewoong’s pipeline.

Key Findings

1. Upfronts vary 8x by modality: Immunology bispecifics ($450M median) vs. oncology small molecules ($50M). Modality selection is the single biggest pricing lever.

2. Regional discount stacking: Ex-China rights price at ~60% of global. Japan ~48%. APAC ~38%. Regional carve-outs can be additive — or dilutive if poorly sequenced.

3. Metabolic/obesity dominates total deal values: Peptide metabolic assets command $156M median upfront with $2B+ total deals. This is the highest-premium space in early-stage licensing.

4. Korean/Asian-origin assets are no longer discounted: CSPC→Madrigal ($120M/$2.1B), Regor→Lilly ($60M/$1.7B), and Daewoong’s own Niroda deal ($506M) demonstrate that Asian-origin PCC-to-IND assets can match or exceed Western benchmarks when the science is differentiated.

4 Regional Pricing — 10 Territories

How Upfront Payments Scale by Territory

Territory scope is the second-most-important pricing lever after modality. Includes observed data for 7 regions with sufficient deal volume, plus directional estimates for 3 emerging territories based on IQVIA pharmaceutical revenue share modeling.

Key Insight

For a Korean pharma out-licensing globally: always negotiate global rights first, then carve out Korea. Global rights yield $110M median upfront vs. $67M for ex-China or $53M for Japan-only. The premium for retaining full territorial scope is significant. If a partner insists on regional rights, stack deals — but be aware that summing regional upfronts rarely equals global economics due to coordination costs and partner competition effects.

Upside Factors (push pricing higher)
  • Novel delivery mechanism (microneedle patch)

  • Multiple potential indications (platform)

Downside Factors (compress pricing)

7 Deal Structure Guide

Typical PCC-to-IND Deal Architecture

How deals are structured at the preclinical-to-IND stage. These are market norms, not prescriptions — specific terms will depend on asset quality, competitive dynamics, and negotiation leverage.

Key Insight

Upfront-to-total ratios vary dramatically by modality: Bispecific immunology deals have 46% upfront (partners pay for conviction), while small molecule metabolic deals are only 6% upfront (most value in back-loaded milestones tied to massive commercial potential). For Daewoong’s GLP-1 patch, expect a low upfront ratio (8–12%) but very large total deal value ($1.5B–$2.2B). Negotiate hard on development milestone triggers, not just upfront.

Option Structures

Approximately 15–20% of PCC-to-IND deals include an option component — the licensee pays a smaller upfront for the right to exercise a full license at a later data inflection point. For Daewoong, consider offering opt-in rights at Phase 1 data readout to maximize upfront while preserving optionality for partners.

Option Deal (Phase 1 readout)

Partner pays smaller upfront + R&D funding, then decides at Phase 1 data.

Upfront: 40–60% of full license upfront
Option fee: 15–30% of remaining
Exercise: Full milestones + royalties kick in
Full License (Standard)

Partner takes full commercial rights from signing. More common for hot assets.

Methodology Notes
  • Stage classification: “PCC-to-IND” includes preclinical candidate selection, discovery stage, IND-enabling, and Phase 1 (first-in-human). Assets already in Phase 2+ are excluded.
  • Financial terms: Only deals with publicly disclosed upfront payments and/or total deal values are included. Undisclosed deals are excluded to avoid survivorship bias.
  • Currency: All values in USD. Non-USD deals converted at announcement-date exchange rates.
  • Total deal value: Upfront + development milestones + commercial milestones + equity (where disclosed). Royalty values are not capitalized into total deal value.
  • Regional estimates: Australia/NZ, LATAM, and GCC values are modeled from observed ratios in the dataset scaled by IQVIA revenue shares, not directly observed transactions.
  • Percentiles: p25 and p75 are linear interpolation percentiles. For cells with n<3, data is suppressed (“—”) to avoid misleading precision.
