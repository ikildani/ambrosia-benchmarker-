Key findings

1. Upfronts vary 5–15× by modality: immunology bispecifics ($450M median) vs oncology PROTACs ($57M).
2. Regional discount from global rights: Ex-China ~60%, Japan ~48%, APAC ~38%, China ~75%, LATAM/GCC ~20–30%.
3. Metabolic/obesity is the hottest space: peptide assets command $156M median upfront with $2B+ total deals (Gubra→AbbVie, Regor→Lilly precedents).
4. siRNAs price at a premium over small molecules for the same indication (differentiated MOA + longer exclusivity).

Relevance to Daewoong’s pipeline

Metabolic / Obesity: Daewoong’s microneedle GLP-1 patch and monthly injectable programs target a $100B+ TAM where single-asset peaks reach $45–50B (Wegovy, Zepbound). Delivery-differentiated GLP-1 assets benchmark against Gubra→AbbVie ($350M/$2.2B) and Lexicon→Novo ($75M/$1B).

Immunology: The DWP213388 experience ($11M upfront) reflects early-stage autoimmune with limited clinical validation. Differentiated bispecifics/peptides in immunology command $150–600M upfront globally when targets are validated.

Korean ex-Asia out-licensing: Asian-origin licensors typically out-license ex-China or ex-Asia rights at 55–65% of global-rights equivalent. iN Therapeutics/Niroda ($506M total for US/EU) anchors the upper end for Korean-origin assets.
