Biomarker-selected assets command a premium

Acrivon's AP3 precision proteomics platform for patient selection is a differentiator in deal negotiations. Our data shows biomarker-selected oncology assets command 20–35% higher upfronts than unselected programs at the same phase, because they de-risk the clinical path and compress timelines to proof-of-concept.
