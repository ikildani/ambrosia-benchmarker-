## Caption Styling Guide

### Primary Headlines
- **Font:** Inter Bold (or SF Pro Bold)
- **Size:** 48-56px
- **Color:** White (#FFFFFF)
- **Position:** Center, lower third or center screen
- **Shadow:** `0 4px 20px rgba(0,0,0,0.5)`

### Secondary Text
- **Font:** Inter Regular
- **Size:** 32-36px
- **Color:** Slate (#94A3B8)

### Accent Text (numbers, key terms)
- **Color:** Teal (#14B8A6)

### Animation
- **In:** Fade up (0.3s, 10px travel)
- **Hold:** 2-3 seconds
- **Out:** Fade (0.2s)
