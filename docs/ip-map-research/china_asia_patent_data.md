# Chinese (and Japanese/Korean) patent data for a biotech patent-intelligence product: sources, licences, costs, entity resolution, and why China matters

Research date: 2026-09-28. Around 25 tool calls. Where a primary source could not be read, this is flagged.

## 1. Market: how much big-pharma in-licensing and M&A comes from China-originated assets (2023-2026)

### Takeaway
Every major tracker shows a steep rise. China-originated assets were about 21% of global out-licensing value in 2023-24 (Jefferies), 28% of large-pharma in-licensed innovator drugs in 2024 (GlobalData), and about a third of licensed molecules and upfronts in 2025 (DealForma, Raymond James, Intralinks/PharmCube). In 2025 China-sourced out-licensing reached about $136-138B in headline value, and the average upfront per China deal is up about 230% since 2022. A licensing-focused IP product that has no CN coverage misses roughly a third of the flow.

### Cited Findings
- Jefferies: China-made assets were 32% of worldwide pharma out-licensing deal value in H1 2025, up from 21% in 2023 and 2024. Jefferies counted 125 China BD deals in 2024 against 65 in 2020. — [Fierce Biotech (Jefferies report)](https://www.fiercebiotech.com/biotech/china-biotechs-reshaping-us-biopharma-outlicensing-deals-rise-11-jefferies-report)
- DealForma: about a third of large pharma's external molecules come from China. China licensing-out deal count rose about 40% and value about 90% versus 2023. Raymond James: about a third of assets large pharma licensed in 2024 were from China. — [Fierce Biotech / search summary](https://www.fiercebiotech.com/biotech/china-biotechs-reshaping-us-biopharma-outlicensing-deals-rise-11-jefferies-report); [BioSpace](https://www.biospace.com/business/big-pharma-rushes-to-china-for-deal-prospecting-despite-regulatory-uncertainty). This comes from the search-result summary. The primary DealForma figures were not read directly.

US licensing of Chinese drugs was $21.3B in 2024 against $15.7B in 2023. mAbs accounted for $11.3B and ADCs for $10.0B. — [Pharmaceutical Technology / GlobalData](https://www.pharmaceutical-technology.com/analyst-comment/large-pharma-drug-licensing-china-2024/)

The underlying data provider is not stated. — [PharmExec](https://www.pharmexec.com/view/deepening-ties-why-china-becoming-big-pharma-most-essential-rd-partner); [Fierce Biotech](https://www.fiercebiotech.com/biotech/analyst-china-no-longer-bargain-basement-biotech-acquisitions)

They were licensor in 39 of 88 global pharma megadeals (44%), more than the US for the first time. Citeline Pharmaprojects: more drugs debuted in China than anywhere else in 2025, the first time the US was overtaken. — [Drug Discovery Trends](https://www.drugdiscoverytrends.com/chinese-firms-landed-6-of-26-major-pharma-deals-in-16-months-worth-53-billion/)

The originating page and provider were not confirmed, so these should be verified before use. — [techlifesci (unverified)](https://www.techlifesci.com/p/big-pharmas-china-deal-wave-and-12)

### Inferences
- The sources measure different things (value vs count, out-licensing vs large-pharma in-licensing, headline vs upfront). All of them land at roughly 25-40% China share in 2025, against about 5-21% in 2021-2024. Any single number quoted should name its provider and its metric.
- Most of these deals are ex-China licences of assets whose priority filings are CN. The first and densest patent record therefore sits at CNIPA, often 12-18+ months before any US/EP publication. That is the core argument for CN-first coverage.

### Gaps
- Stifel and BioCentury China-share figures were not found. J.P. Morgan's JPM 2026 China statistics were not found in primary form.
- China-share figures for M&A specifically (as opposed to licensing) were not found. Intralinks gives total pharma M&A of $141.4B in 2025 (+129%) but no China split.

## 2. CNIPA official data: open data, access, formats, cost

### Takeaway
CNIPA runs free public search and query systems, including cpquery for file-wrapper-style status and PSS for search. Since Dec 2024, cpquery publishes legal status in real-time WIPO ST.27 form. CNIPA also shares 60 "open data" types through its IP data resource public service system. However, standardized bulk data is provided "on a targeted basis" to named institutions (45 in 2024). I found no documented, self-serve, English-language commercial bulk licence for foreign companies.

### Cited Findings
- CNIPA reported (WIPO CWS annual technical report 2024) that open data types grew to 60 via the IP data resource public service system. It "provided standardized data to 45 research institutions, IP service agencies, and enterprises on a targeted basis." — [WIPO CWS ATR 2024 CN](https://confluence.wipo.int/confluence/spaces/ATR/pages/1640996450/CWS+ATR+PI+2024+CN)
- The Patent Search and Analysis system (pss-system.cponline.cnipa.gov.cn) is free and covers worldwide patent data. The China and Global Patent Examination Information Inquiry (cpquery.cponline.cnipa.gov.cn) is free and gives basic, examination, fee, reexamination/invalidation, register and family information. — [WIPO CWS ATR 2024 CN](https://confluence.wipo.int/confluence/spaces/ATR/pages/1640996450/CWS+ATR+PI+2024+CN)
- In Dec 2024 CNIPA upgraded the system to convert its legal-status data in real time to WIPO ST.27-conformant legal status that the public can query. The UI is available in 8 languages. — [WIPO CWS ATR 2024 CN](https://confluence.wipo.int/confluence/spaces/ATR/pages/1640996450/CWS+ATR+PI+2024+CN)
- The gazette is published twice weekly. Publications include bibliographic data, abstracts and abstract drawings, plus transaction data. Internal databases are XML, covering the Patent Gazette since 1985. — [WIPO CWS ATR 2024 CN](https://confluence.wipo.int/confluence/spaces/ATR/pages/1640996450/CWS+ATR+PI+2024+CN)
- In 2024 CNIPA completed the National IP Public Service Platform, a unified portal for patents, trademarks, IC layouts and GIs. Local search bibliographic fields went from 7 to 29, and advanced users can download more fields. — [CNIPA English site / search summary](https://english.cnipa.gov.cn/art/2021/3/15/art_1340_157759.html)
- SureChEMBL (EMBL-EBI) receives translated CN full text (no attachments) from CNIPA. It reports CNIPA as the largest office by volume (about 51M patents) but notes that limited data access yields fewer extracted compounds. — [EMBL-EBI SureChEMBL 2.0](https://www.embl.org/news/updates-from-data-resources/surechembl-2-0-is-here/)

### Inferences
- A practical route to CN full text and claims in English at v1 is indirect: SureChEMBL's chemistry extraction (open, CC-BY-SA per EBI norms, but verify), Google Patents' web pages, or a commercial aggregator. A direct CNIPA bulk agreement is unlikely to be fast for a foreign startup.

### Gaps
- No published fee schedule, licence text or foreign-eligibility rules were found for CNIPA bulk "patent data resources". The Chinese-language portal (知识产权数据资源公共服务系统) terms were not readable in this pass.
- CNIPA's own English machine-translation availability in bulk was not confirmed.
- It was not confirmed whether cpquery ToS allow automated querying. Treat that as a risk.

## 3. Aggregators: EPO, Google, Lens, WIPO, JPO, KIPRIS

### Takeaway
The cheapest legally clean base is EPO DOCDB + INPADOC. These have been free bulk downloads since Jan-Feb 2025 and cover CN/JP/KR bibliographic data, families, citations and legal events. Add Google Patents Public Data on BigQuery (CC BY 4.0, commercial use and redistribution allowed with attribution) for machine-translated English titles and abstracts and original-language CN assignee names. Claims and descriptions in the Google public tables were documented as US-only. WIPO PATENTSCOPE commercial redistribution requires a paid licence. Lens commercial use requires a paid, custom-priced licence. KIPRIS Plus Open API is $1,783/yr. JPO offers bulk download on application.

### Cited Findings
**EPO**
- EPO made seven datasets free from 1 Jan 2025 in the BDDS Public Area, with no subscription or authentication. DOCDB (worldwide bibliographic) and INPADOC (worldwide legal events) have been available since 17 Feb 2025, EP full text since 10 Feb 2025. — [patent.dev](https://patent.dev/game-changer-key-epo-patent-datasets-are-now-free/)
- INPADOC legal status is compiled from gazettes and registers of about 100 countries/organisations, with more than 470M legal-status entries (Oct 2024). DOCDB covers 100+ offices. — [Espacenet help](https://worldwide.espacenet.com/help?locale=en_ep&method=handleHelpTopic&topic=legalstatusqh); [pkg.go.dev epo-ops](https://pkg.go.dev/github.com/patent-dev/epo-ops)
- OPS non-paying users get 4 GB/week. OPS T&Cs (v2.0) let users put data "in their own machine-readable databases, products and services" and distribute it as part of those products, but forbid making "the data as such available to the public". No royalties are due (cl. 3.1-3.2, 4.6). — [EPO OPS T&Cs PDF](https://developers.epo.org/sites/default/files/terms_and_conditions_OPS%202.0%20EN_DE_FR.pdf) (as quoted in [GitHub issue summarising verified terms](https://github.com/bucket-foundation/x402-research-gateway/issues/68))
- The BDDS raw-data (DOCDB/INPADOC) licence terms were not verified. They are at [EPO raw data T&Cs](https://www.epo.org/service-support/ordering/raw-data-terms-and-conditions.html). — [GitHub issue](https://github.com/bucket-foundation/x402-research-gateway/issues/68)

**Google Patents Public Datasets (BigQuery)**
- Licence: CC BY 4.0 ("Google Patents Public Data by IFI CLAIMS Patent Services and Google"). Commercial use and redistribution are allowed with attribution. The BigQuery sandbox is capped at 1 TiB of queries per month. — [GitHub issue summarising terms](https://github.com/bucket-foundation/x402-research-gateway/issues/68); [Google Cloud blog](https://cloud.google.com/blog/topics/public-datasets/google-patents-public-datasets-connecting-public-paid-and-private-patent-data)
- `patents.publications`: claims_localized and description_localized are "for US publications only". Titles and abstracts are localized for all countries. The table has citation and priority_claim records. The 2018 snapshot documents CN at 19.2M publications with localized titles and abstracts but no full-text claims or descriptions, JP at about 24.4M and KR at about 4.8M. `google_patents_research.publications` adds English titles and abstracts machine-translated by Google Translate. — [google/patents-public-data table docs](https://github.com/google/patents-public-data/blob/master/tables/dataset_Google%20Patents%20Public%20Datasets.md)
- An academic validation reports that Google Patents Public Data covers 99.96% of the official Chinese patent universe, with high field integrity for core metadata and examiner data. IPC is missing for 23%, and there are limits on entity disambiguation, MT quality and reporting lags. — [ResearchGate: "Chinese Patent in Google Patent Public Data: A Guide to Processing and Validation"](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/401747417_Chinese_Patent_in_Google_Patent_Public_Data_A_Guide_to_Processing_and_Validation) (the page returned 403; figures are from the search snippet)
- Chen & Esposito (arXiv 2606.26470) took all CNIPA invention patents with English MT titles and abstracts from Google Patents, and families from PATSTAT. — [arXiv 2606.26470](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2606.26470)

**WIPO PATENTSCOPE**
- Commercial use requires a paid data licence with "added value". Bulk redistribution requires a paid licence, and all three PCT data licences carry fees (terms updated 2025-11-12). — [WIPO PATENTSCOPE data terms](https://www.wipo.int/en/web/patentscope/data/terms) (via [GitHub issue](https://github.com/bucket-foundation/x402-research-gateway/issues/68))

**Lens.org**
- Lens offers a commercial use licence agreement for its platform and metadata. API pricing depends on use case, attribution plan and volume/speed. Trial access is non-commercial only, and commercial permission requires a custom API plan. — [Lens Patent & Scholar API](https://support.lens.org/knowledge-base/lens-patent-and-scholar-api/); [Lens API Terms](https://about.lens.org/lens-api-terms-of-use/)
- Professional users are expected to pay about US$1,000-5,000/yr for the interface. APIs and bulk patent-sequence access are extra subscriptions. — [WIPO Manual on Open Source Patent Analytics, ch. 9](https://wipo-analytics.github.io/manual/the-lens-1.html)

**JPO (Japan)**
- JPO provides a Bulk Data Download Site for the latest published data, plus archived data on external storage. Archived-media applications resumed 1 Aug 2025. Applications go by email to PA0630@jpo.go.jp. J-PlatPat is free, and its CSV export is limited to 100 or fewer hits. — [JPO Bulk Download Service](https://www.jpo.go.jp/e/system/laws/koho/internet/download.html); [JPO bulk data](https://www.jpo.go.jp/e/system/patent/gaiyo/seido-bunrui/bulkdata.html)

**KIPRIS Plus (Korea)**
- The Open API is a flat $1,783/yr and includes up to 1,000 searches/month. Bulk (fixed-term) data is priced per a separate table. Contact: kiprisplus@kipi.or.kr. — [KIPRIS Plus Service Fee](https://plus.kipris.or.kr/eng/use/paymentMmg.do?menuNo=310105)
- Discounts: 50% for 2 or fewer Open API products, plus an additional 50% for individuals, SMEs, public and non-profit users. — [KIPRIS Plus fees (search summary)](https://plus.kipris.or.kr/portal/use/paymentMmg.do?menuNo=210112)

### Inferences
- For CN claims and full text in English, the free and clean options are thin. Google's public BigQuery table did not include CN claims (per the docs). The Google Patents website shows CN full text with MT, but scraping it is a ToS risk. Commercial full-text CN usually means a paid vendor (IFI CLAIMS, LexisNexis PatentSight/TotalPatent, Clarivate DWPI, PatSnap), a Lens commercial licence, or selective per-document retrieval via EPO OPS (the OPS "full-text" service covers only some offices; CN full-text availability in OPS was not confirmed).
- The DOCDB + Google combination gives a CC-BY/EPO-licensed spine: families, priority links to US/EP/WO, CN legal events via INPADOC, MT abstracts and original CN assignee names.

### Gaps
- It was not verified whether Google's public `publications` table has since gained non-US claims/description (the docs date from 2018). This should be checked directly in BigQuery before building on it.
- EPO BDDS raw-data licence clauses for commercial redistribution were not read.
- JPO bulk commercial-use terms and fees were not found. KIPRIS bulk price table was not displayed.
- Lens commercial price points for API/bulk are not public.

## 4. Citation data for CN patents

### Takeaway
CN citation data exists (examiner citations appear in CN search reports and flow to DOCDB/Google), but it is not comparable to USPTO data. CN citations are biased toward Chinese-language literature, have limited coverage before about 2010, and CN examiners tend to cite domestic prior art. Only about 1% of CNIPA patents sit in families also filed at USPTO/EPO, where citation practice is standardized. Cross-office family linkage via DOCDB therefore works for the small, high-value subset that licensing actually targets.

### Cited Findings
- "CNIPA citations are institutionally biased toward Chinese-language papers and have limited coverage before 2010… The common workaround is to restrict attention to the CNIPA patents also filed at the USPTO or EPO, where citation practices are standardized. Yet these patent families capture only 1% of CNIPA patents." — [Chen & Esposito, arXiv 2606.26470](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2606.26470)
- Examiners tend to cite home-country prior art, and national examination guidelines produce different citation counts. Asian-office citation coverage in PATSTAT is incomplete for some historical periods. — [search summary of patent-citation literature](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0172219026000566); [arXiv 2603.17881](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2603.17881)
- Using international-search-report (ISR) citations, Chinese PCT applications reach only about a third of the non-Chinese quality benchmark on foreign citations. Including domestic and self citations raises the measured quality. — [Boeing & Mueller, ISR indices (ScienceDirect)](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S1043951X19300926)
- Chinese invention-patent citations include both inventor-added and examiner-added citations, which studies analyse separately. — [Huang et al. 2021, SAGE](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/abs/10.1177/0971721820932020)
- Google Patents Public Data carries CN examiner information with high field integrity. — [ResearchGate guide (snippet)](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/401747417_Chinese_Patent_in_Google_Patent_Public_Data_A_Guide_to_Processing_and_Validation)

### Inferences
- For a licensing product, the relevant CN assets are those with ex-China licences, which almost always have PCT/US/EP family members. Use DOCDB family IDs to attach the US/EP/WO (ISR) citation sets to CN priority documents rather than relying on CN-native citation counts. CN-native forward citations should not be mixed into US-calibrated citation indicators without per-office normalization.

### Gaps
- No precise statistic was found for the share of CN documents in DOCDB with citation records, by year.

## 5. Entity resolution for Chinese assignees

### Takeaway
This is the hardest part. Google's harmonized (romanized) assignee field is populated for fewer than 1 in 5 CNIPA patents and is essentially absent after 2015. Original Chinese-language applicant names, however, are present for virtually every CNIPA patent. The robust approach resolves on the Chinese-character name, mapped to the Unified Social Credit Code, company registry and subsidiary trees, plus curated tables for universities and hospitals. It should not rely on pinyin or English strings.

### Cited Findings
- Google's "romanized, harmonized assignee field is populated for fewer than one in five CNIPA patents and is essentially absent after 2015, whereas Chinese-language assignee names are available for virtually every CNIPA patent." — [Chen & Esposito, arXiv 2606.26470](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2606.26470)
- Applicant-name standardization is an obstacle nearly all researchers hit when using or linking Chinese patent data. — [search summary, Scientometrics literature](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11192-019-03310-w)
- Large-scale disambiguation of SIPO inventors 1985-2016 found 3.99M unique inventors from 1.84M Chinese names across 14.68M patent-inventor records. That is a heavy name-collision problem. — [Scientometrics 2019](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11192-019-03310-w)
- General assignee-consolidation algorithms (e.g. "Terrorizer") address subsidiary and name-variant undercounting of multinationals. — [Scientometrics 2025 / arXiv 2403.12083](https://arxiv.org/html/2403.12083v1)
- KU Leuven ECOOM's patentee-harmonization methodology is the basis of PATSTAT's EEE-PPAT / PSN harmonized names, presented at WIPO CWS. — [Callaert, WIPO](https://www.wipo.int/edocs/mdocs/classifications/en/wipo_ip_cws_ns_ge_19/wipo_ip_cws_ns_part_1_callaert.pdf)

### Inferences
- Recommended pipeline:
  1. Key on the original CN applicant string (中文).
  2. Normalize full-width/half-width characters, 有限公司/股份有限公司 suffixes and province/city prefixes (江苏/上海).
  3. Map to parent groups using a hand-curated seed list of about 300-500 biopharma groups, for example Hengrui (江苏恒瑞医药 + 上海恒瑞 + 成都盛迪 subsidiaries), CSPC, Hansoh, Akeso, 3SBio, BeiGene, Innovent, Kelun and RemeGen.
  4. Use HKEX/SSE STAR prospectus subsidiary lists as sources.
  5. Add separate curated tables for universities and their affiliated hospitals (e.g. 复旦大学附属中山医院), and for CAS institutes.
  6. Use DOCDB family links to borrow the English/USPTO assignee name from the US family member.
- This curated China biopharma entity table is a defensible moat asset, because the free sources do not supply it.

### Gaps
- No open, maintained China-specific pharma assignee-to-company concordance was found. Commercial alternatives were not priced: PatSnap, PatentSight, Qichacha/Tianyancha registry APIs.

## 6. Pharma-specific Chinese regimes: patent linkage, patent term compensation, data exclusivity

### Takeaway
China now has three layers that matter for asset valuation, and all three generate public records a product can ingest:
1. Patent linkage (since July 2021) with a public NMPA patent-information registration platform, similar to an Orange Book.
2. New-drug patent term compensation (Patent Law Art. 42(3), in force 1 Jun 2021, operational from 20 Jan 2024): up to 5 years, with a cap of 14 years of effective term after approval.
3. Regulatory data exclusivity: final NMPA Implementation Measures issued and effective 15 May 2026, giving 6 years for Class 1 innovative drugs and biologics and certain overseas-originated products.

### Cited Findings
- Patent linkage: NMPA and CNIPA announced the Implementing Measures for the Early Resolution Mechanism for Drug Patent Disputes (Trial) on 4 Jul 2021, implementing Patent Law Art. 76. CNIPA issued separate administrative-adjudication measures. NMPA invited brand companies to list patents on the registration platform. — [CMS](https://cms.law/en/chn/legal-updates/China-makes-patent-linkage-system-officially-available); [Finnegan](https://www.finnegan.com/en/insights/articles/comparative-overview-of-drug-patent-linkage-systems-in-china-and-the-united-states.html)
- By 13 Sep 2021 the China Listed Drug Patent Information Registration Platform held registrations for 527 chemical drugs, 77 biosimilars and 239 TCMs. There were 323 patent declarations under 279 generic applications: 234 Type I, 14 Type II, 40 Type III, 15 Type IV-1 and 20 Type IV-2. — [Jones Day / Lexology summary](https://www.jonesday.com/en/insights/2021/09/patent-linkage-and-article-76-proceedings-in-china-a-litigators-perspective)
- Patent term compensation: CNIPA grants extension on request for invention patents on drugs approved in China, to compensate for trial and approval time. The extension is capped at 5 years and total effective term after approval at 14 years. The law has been in force since 1 Jun 2021, and the implementing regulations and examination guidelines since 20 Jan 2024. — [Bird & Bird](https://www.twobirds.com/en/insights/2025/china/chinas-pharmaceutical-patent-term-extension-cases); [AWA](https://www.awa.com/awapoint/everything-you-need-to-know-about-chinas-patent-term-extension-pte/)
- In June 2025 CNIPA granted 1,827 days (the 5-year maximum) for telitacicept's core patent. — [Sonderhoff & Einsel](https://se1910.com/chinas-first-5-year-extension-case-under-the-patent-term-compensation-system/)
- Data exclusivity: NMPA issued the draft Interim Implementation Regulations for Drug Trial Data Exclusivity on 19 Mar 2025, with comments closing 18 May 2025. The draft proposed up to 6 years, reduced for overseas-first approvals by the lag. — [Bird & Bird 2025](https://www.twobirds.com/en/insights/2025/china/china-releases-comprehensive-draft-data-exclusivity-rules-for-comments)
- NMPA issued the final Implementation Measures for Drug Trial Data Protection on 15 May 2026, effective immediately. They give 6 years from MA approval for Class 1 innovative chemical drugs and biologics and Class 5.1/3.1 overseas-originated products. The request must be filed with the MAA. Pending applicants had to file with CDE within 15 days (by 5 Jun 2026). — [Ropes & Gray, May 2026](https://www.ropesgray.com/en/insights/alerts/2026/05/chinas-nmpa-issues-final-measures-on-regulatory-data-protection); [Bird & Bird 2026](https://www.twobirds.com/en/insights/2026/china/china-reveals-long-awaited-regulatory-data-protection-rules); [China Patent Strategy](https://chinapatentstrategy.com/breaking-china-released-drug-trial-data-protection-implementation-measures/)

### Inferences
- A v1 CN "exclusivity stack" per asset can be built from four records: the CNIPA patent (DOCDB/INPADOC), the linkage-platform listing (brand-declared patents, the Orange-Book analogue), the PTE grant (CNIPA legal events/gazette) and the NMPA data-protection publication. This mirrors the US Orange Book + PTE + NCE model and is a differentiator.

### Gaps
- Current (2025-26) counts of linkage-platform listings, Article 76 cases or total PTE grants were not found. The platform's bulk/export terms were not checked.
- It was not confirmed whether the final 2026 measures kept the "6 years minus overseas lag" haircut.

## 7. Practical recommendation: minimal viable CN/JP/KR stack for v1 and its cost

### Takeaway
A v1 can reach usable CN/JP/KR coverage for close to $0 in licence fees plus modest compute, with about $2k/yr added for Korea. The components are:
- EPO DOCDB + INPADOC bulk (free) as the spine.
- Google Patents Public Data on BigQuery (CC BY 4.0) for English MT titles and abstracts, CN-language assignees and citations.
- A curated China biopharma entity table (built in-house).
- The NMPA linkage platform, PTE and data-exclusivity records (public web).
- KIPRIS Plus API ($1,783/yr).
- JPO bulk (free by application, terms to confirm).

English full-text CN claims are the main gap and would need a paid vendor or a Lens commercial licence.

### Cited Findings
- DOCDB/INPADOC are free via BDDS since 2025. — [patent.dev](https://patent.dev/game-changer-key-epo-patent-datasets-are-now-free/)
- Google Patents Public Data is CC BY 4.0, commercial use allowed. — [GitHub issue summarising terms](https://github.com/bucket-foundation/x402-research-gateway/issues/68)
- KIPRIS Open API costs $1,783/yr. — [KIPRIS Plus](https://plus.kipris.or.kr/eng/use/paymentMmg.do?menuNo=310105)
- Lens professional pricing is about $1-5k/yr, with API/bulk extra and custom-priced. — [WIPO manual](https://wipo-analytics.github.io/manual/the-lens-1.html)
- WIPO PATENTSCOPE commercial redistribution requires paid licences. — [WIPO terms](https://www.wipo.int/en/web/patentscope/data/terms)

### Inferences
- v1 stack (estimated cost):
  1. EPO BDDS DOCDB + INPADOC weekly: $0 licence, plus storage and ETL (low hundreds of $/month).
  2. EPO OPS for on-demand per-document lookups within the 4 GB/week free tier: $0.
  3. Google BigQuery patents tables, filtered once to IPC A61K/A61P/C07K/C12N and CN/JP/KR, then exported: pay-per-query compute. This is likely tens to low hundreds of $ per full refresh; CC BY attribution is required.
  4. SureChEMBL for CN chemistry, if relevant.
  5. NMPA/CDE public pages for the linkage, PTE and data-exclusivity registers (engineering only).
  6. KIPRIS Plus: $1,783/yr.
  7. Curated entity table: analyst time, the real cost.
- Next tier (v1.5): a commercial CN full-text/claims licence (Lens custom plan or IFI CLAIMS). Expect four to low-five figures per year, but these prices are not public and must be quoted.
- CN coverage is the priority over JP/KR because deal share is concentrated in China. JP/KR licensors (e.g. Korean ADC/bispecific players, Japanese pharma) are real but a smaller share. The data sources cited above do not break out JP/KR deal share.

### Gaps
- Actual BigQuery cost for the patents tables was not measured.
- Vendor quotes for CN full-text English claims (IFI, Clarivate, PatSnap, Lens commercial) were not obtained.
- The EPO BDDS raw-data licence should be read before redistributing DOCDB-derived fields to customers.
