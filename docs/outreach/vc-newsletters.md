# VC & Investor Newsletter Outreach

## Strategy

VCs are not link builders — they're force multipliers. One VC partner mentioning your tool in their newsletter or blog reaches hundreds of portfolio companies, BD teams, and other investors. The goal is:
1. Get the tool into portfolio company hands (they become paying users)
2. Get mentioned in the partner's blog/newsletter (high-authority backlink)
3. Get cited in board-level deal discussions (word-of-mouth flywheel)

**All VC outreach is handled by Issa** — these are relationship plays, not volume plays.

| John LaMattina | Forbes contributor | Former Pfizer R&D head. Writes about pharma deals. |

**Engagement playbook (intern):**
1. Follow all of the above
2. Turn on notifications for their posts
3. When they mention any deal, reply within 30 minutes with: "The benchmark data on this — [specific stat from your platform]. Full data: [link]"
4. Never be salesy. Just be the data source that shows up consistently.
