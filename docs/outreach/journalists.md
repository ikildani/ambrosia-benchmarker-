## How to Find Email Addresses

1. **Check their published articles** — many include contact email in byline
2. **Twitter/X bio** — journalists often list email or "DMs open"
3. **LinkedIn** — InMail for Tier 1 targets
4. **Standard patterns:** firstname.lastname@outlet.com, firstinitiallastname@outlet.com
5. **Hunter.io** — verify email patterns for each outlet
6. **Press pages** — most outlets have a "Contact" or "Tips" page with editorial email
