The attached Deal Terms Benchmark is a working sample of the Term Validation deliverable, run on the NewCo economics in your June 2026 materials. It shows that your proposed upfront sits materially below the range at which comparable China-origin oncology assets have cleared — and quantifies what the retained equity stake must be worth for the NewCo route to compensate for that difference.

That question is worth more than this engagement costs. Transactions in this size range typically fall below the engagement threshold of bulge-bracket advisors, which is precisely why assets of this quality are so often underpriced: the analytical rigor that produces a defensible number is usually reserved for transactions ten times larger. This engagement delivers that rigor, augmented by deal intelligence infrastructure large banks do not maintain.

Standard terms

  • Ambrosia acts as exclusive transaction advisor for the assets in scope during Phase 2 only. Phase 1 carries no exclusivity.
  • Travel expenses, if required, billed at cost with prior approval.
