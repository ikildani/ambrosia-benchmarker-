## Production Tips

### For Screen Recordings:
- Use Loom, OBS, or QuickTime for clean captures
- Set browser to 1920x1080, zoom to 110% for readability
- Hide bookmarks bar, extensions, and other tabs
- Use dark mode on the site for more cinematic look (the navy backgrounds photograph well)
- Record at 60fps for smooth cursor movement
- Add a yellow circle highlight effect around the cursor for click emphasis
- Slow down cursor movements — don't rush between clicks

### For Talking Head:
- Good lighting (ring light or window light, avoid overhead fluorescents)
- Clean background or blurred office
- Look at camera lens, not screen
- Keep energy up but professional (this is biopharma, not fitness)
- Wear solid colors (navy, white, teal match the brand)

### For Text Animation:
- Use Canva or CapCut templates
- Navy background (#0c0e1f) with white text for primary content
- Teal (#0EA5A5) for accent/highlight words, stats, and numbers
- Cyan (#06B6D4) for secondary accents
- Plus Jakarta Sans or Inter font (match your site)
- Add subtle transitions between cards (fade or slide, not flashy)
- Keep text large enough to read on mobile (minimum 24pt for key text)

### Posting Schedule:
- LinkedIn: 3-4 posts per week (Tue, Wed, Thu mornings EST)
- Instagram Stories: Daily or every other day
- Reels/Videos: 1-2 per week
- Best times: 7-9 AM EST (BD teams check LinkedIn before meetings)
- Market Pulse update video: every Monday morning

### Thumbnail Tips:
- Always include a human face if possible (3x more clicks)
- Use navy background with one bold teal stat/number
- Add 3-5 words max on the thumbnail
- Include the Ambrosia logo in the corner
