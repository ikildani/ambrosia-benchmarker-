## VIDEO EDITING TIPS

1. **Speed up mouse movements** — 1.5x-2x speed during navigation, normal speed when showing results
2. **Add zoom effects** — zoom into specific numbers/charts when they appear (Ken Burns style)
3. **Cursor highlight** — use a cursor highlighter plugin so viewers can track where you click
4. **Transitions:** Use quick crossfades (0.2s) between scenes, not fancy transitions
5. **Lower thirds:** Use consistent bottom-left text overlays (white text, dark semi-transparent background)
6. **Final card:** 3-second hold on CTA with URL
7. **Aspect ratio:** Record in 1920×1080, then create a 1080×1920 version by cropping + zooming for Instagram
8. **File format:** Export as MP4, H.264, at least 30fps
9. **LinkedIn specs:** Max 10 min, recommended under 90 sec, max 5GB
10. **Instagram Reels specs:** Max 90 sec, 1080×1920, max 4GB
