## Post 7: Educational (Thought Leadership)

5 things I learned analyzing 600+ biopharma licensing deals:

1. Phase matters more than modality for upfront size
Phase 3 assets command 3-5x the upfront of Phase 1 assets in the same therapeutic area. De-risking is the single biggest value driver.

2. Oncology still dominates deal volume, but neurology is catching up
88+ R&D partnerships worth $45.9B in neurology alone (2024-2026). The CNS renaissance is real.

3. Territory scope drives total deal value more than any single financial term
Global rights vs. regional rights can swing total deal value by 40-60%.

4. Royalty rates are stickier than upfronts
Upfronts vary widely deal-to-deal. Royalties cluster tightly within modality bands. Know your modality's range.

5. The best-prepared team wins the negotiation
Teams that walk in with comparable deal data consistently negotiate better terms. Data is leverage.

All of these insights come from our database of 600+ real biopharma transactions.
