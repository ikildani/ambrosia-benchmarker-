How the platform thinks

  1. Ingest · daily

    SEC EDGAR full text since 2017, Hong Kong exchange filings, press wires, FDA and ClinicalTrials.gov.

  2. Verify · cite or exclude

    Every term is scored and linked to its filing. Anything below 75/100 confidence stays out of the models.

  3. Model · 24 engines

    Risk-adjusted NPV, comparable scoring, buyer premiums and live macro rates, run under correlated uncertainty.

  4. Decide · ranges, not points

    A memo with a value band, its comparables and the assumptions that move it most.

  5. Learn · weekly backtest

    Estimates are scored against real outcomes, including 164 approved-drug trajectories, then recalibrated.

Standards we hold

  • Every number traces to a source. Each deal term is stored with the filing or record it came from.
  • We publish our error. Backtest accuracy is shown on a live page, including where the models miss.
  • Enterprise controls from day one. Single sign-on, full audit logging and scoped API keys for every user.
