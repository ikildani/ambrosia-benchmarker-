## WHAT NOT TO DO
- Don't send all 50 on the same day (spam filters + you can't handle 50 replies at once)
- Don't attach anything. No PDFs, no decks, no screenshots.
- Don't ask for a call in the first email. The CTA is "try the tool" — zero friction.
- Don't CC anyone. One-to-one only.
- Don't use a mail merge tool for these 50. Type each one. They'll know if it's automated.
- Don't follow up more than twice. If they don't respond after 2 follow-ups, move on.
