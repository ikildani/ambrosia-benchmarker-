2. **CROs/CDMOs guard individual emails** behind corporate contact forms. The best approach is reaching their BD teams through general inquiry forms or LinkedIn outreach.

3. **Many incubators and accelerators** use contact forms rather than publishing emails. LabCentral (info@labcentral.org) and BioCity UK (enquiries.nottingham@biocity.co.uk) are exceptions.

4. **TTOs are the most accessible segment** -- university staff emails often follow predictable patterns (firstname.lastname@university.edu) and are frequently listed on public team pages.

5. **National biotech associations are excellent force multipliers** -- a single partnership could expose the tool to hundreds of member companies. Prioritize Swiss Biotech, BIA UK, HollandBIO, BIO Deutschland, and KoreaBIO.

6. **WebFetch was blocked** during this research, which limited my ability to scrape individual contact pages for specific email addresses. Visiting the team/contact pages of these organizations directly would yield additional individual emails.
